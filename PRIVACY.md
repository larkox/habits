# Privacy Policy for Habits

**Effective date: September 21, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how the **Habits** mobile application (the “App”) handles information. Habits is developed and maintained by the Habits developer (“we,” “us,” or “our”).

## Summary

Habits is designed to work without an account or a developer-operated online service. The information that you enter in the App is stored locally on your device. We do not collect that information on our servers, sell it, use it for advertising, or use analytics or tracking technologies.

## Information handled by the App

You may choose to enter and manage the following information:

- Habit names, schedules, completion dates, and completion history;
- Chart names, recorded values, and the dates associated with those values;
- Todo names and due dates;
- Food or other fridge-item names and expiry dates; and
- Birthday names and dates, including birth years when provided.

The App also creates random internal identifiers for these records and stores basic backup metadata, such as the backup format version and export date, when you export a backup.

Some of this content may identify you or another person depending on what you enter. You are responsible for ensuring that you have an appropriate reason and any necessary permission to store information about other people in the App.

## How information is used

The App uses the information you enter only to provide its features, including displaying and editing your records, calculating schedules and reminders shown inside the App, presenting charts, and creating or restoring backups at your request.

The App does not require an account. We do not collect precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, audio, advertising identifiers, payment information, or authentication information. The App does not contain advertising or developer-operated analytics.

## Local storage and security

App records are stored in a SQLite database within the App's private storage on your device. Access to that storage is controlled by the security features of your device and operating system. We do not receive or have access to the contents of that database.

No method of electronic storage is completely secure. You should protect access to your device and install operating-system security updates when available.

## Backups, imports, and sharing

The App lets you create a JSON backup. You can choose to export either the main records only or the main records together with habit and chart history. Exported backup files are not encrypted by the App and may contain all corresponding information described above.

When you export a backup, the App creates a temporary file and opens the operating system's share interface. The destination app or service that you select receives the file and handles it under its own privacy policy. The App attempts to remove its temporary copy after the share operation.

When you import a backup, you select a JSON file through the operating system's document picker. The App reads the selected file to append its records or replace the records already stored in the App. The App does not upload imported files to us.

Android may back up App data through its system backup features when those features are enabled for your device and Google account. Any such backup is controlled by Android and your account settings and is handled under Google's applicable terms and privacy policy. You can manage device backups through your Android or Google account settings.

## Information sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your App records with third parties. Information leaves the App only when you intentionally export a backup to a destination you select, or when your operating system performs a device backup according to your settings.

The App uses third-party software libraries to provide its interface, local database, document selection, file handling, and sharing functions. We do not use these libraries to send your App records to us or for advertising or analytics.

We may disclose information if required by law, but because we do not receive or control your locally stored App records, we ordinarily cannot access or disclose them.

## Data retention and deletion

Records remain on your device until you delete them in the App, replace the App's data by importing a backup, clear the App's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the App. Uninstalling the App or clearing its storage deletes the App's local database, subject to any copies that may remain in exported files, device backups, or services to which you chose to share a backup.

You control exported backup files and must delete them from their saved locations or third-party services separately. Device-backup copies are controlled through your Android or Google account settings.

Because Habits does not provide user accounts and we do not hold your App records on our systems, there is no account or server-side data for us to delete on your behalf.

## Children's privacy

Habits is a general-purpose organization tool and is not directed specifically to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other user on our systems because the App does not transmit App records to us. Parents and guardians should supervise any child's use of the App and the information entered into it.

## International use

We do not transfer your App records to our systems in any country. A service you choose for an exported backup, or an operating-system backup provider, may process that file in other countries under that provider's privacy policy.

## Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features or data practices change or when required by law. We will update the effective date above and make the revised policy available through the same public location. If a change materially affects how the App handles information, we will provide any additional notice or consent required by applicable law.
